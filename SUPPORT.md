# Support

Forkcast is a free app made by one person in their spare time. Help is best effort: there's no support team and no guaranteed response time, but every report is read.

## First, check Help

**Settings › Help** in the app answers the common questions: logging, the daily budget, AI services, Health Connect, backups and updates. The [install guide](INSTALL.md) covers installing, updating and backups.

## Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub: in the app, **Settings › About › Report a problem**, or **[New issue](https://github.com/djemes/forkcast/issues/new/choose)** on this page. You need a free GitHub account.

A good report includes:
- **Forkcast version:** Settings › About shows it (for example 2.0.0)
- **Android version and phone model:** in your phone's settings, under About phone
- **What you did, step by step,** what you expected, and what happened instead
- A **screenshot**, if it helps. Check that it shows no personal health data or keys first

### If Forkcast crashed

After a crash, the Today screen offers to save a short report. Tap **Save report…**, save the file, and attach it to the issue (drag it into the text box, or use "Paste, drop, or click to add files"). The report holds only the app and Android versions, the phone model and where in the code it stopped: no meals, notes or keys. Nothing is sent unless you attach it.

## Please don't post

- **API keys** or tokens. If you posted one by accident, revoke it at the provider and make a new one
- Your **health data**, food log or backup files
- Anything else personal

Issues are public. For a security problem, don't open an issue: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report it privately.

## Ideas

Feature ideas are welcome as issues too (choose **Feature idea**). Forkcast is deliberately small, so not every idea will make it in.
