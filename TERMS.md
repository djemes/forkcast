## 1. What Forkcast is

Forkcast is a free, open-source app for logging food, water, and weight, and for estimating a daily calorie budget based on the information you enter. It can optionally read and write some data in Android's Health Connect, look up foods in public food databases, and, if you set it up, send meal photos or descriptions to an AI service you choose to estimate their nutrition.

## 2. What Forkcast is not

- Not medical, nutritional, or health advice. Forkcast doesn't diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it isn't a substitute for a doctor, registered dietitian, or other qualified professional.
- Not suitable for everyone. Don't rely on Forkcast without professional guidance if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, have or have had an eating disorder, or have a medical condition (such as diabetes, kidney disease, or heart disease) or take medication affected by what you eat. If calorie tracking makes you feel anxious, preoccupied with food, or unwell, stop using the app and talk to a professional.
- Not exact. Calorie budgets come from standard formulas and your own data. Food values come from public databases, product labels, your entries, and AI estimates. All of these can be incomplete or wrong. Check anything important, for example by weighing food or reading the label.
- Not an emergency service. If you feel unwell, contact a medical professional or emergency services.
