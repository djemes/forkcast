Privacy policy

Forkcast for Android · Effective October 25, 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone. Forkcast has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your data. Forkcast only connects to services you choose to set up, and only to do what you asked.

What Forkcast stores on your phone

Your food log, foods and recipes, weight entries, goals, settings and meal photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. API keys and tokens you enter (for example for an AI service, the USDA food database or GitHub) are encrypted on the device with the Android Keystore.

Services Forkcast connects to

Forkcast only sends data to the services below, and only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Food databases: when you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts and, if you added a key, to the USDA FoodData Central API.
  • AI photo estimates (optional): when you estimate a meal from a photo, the photo (with location and other metadata removed), your description, and any personal context you entered in Settings are sent to the AI service you configured with your own API key (for example Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or an OpenAI-compatible service). That service's own privacy policy and terms apply. Some free tiers, such as Gemini's, allow the provider to use what you send to improve its products.
  • Health Connect (optional): see below.
  • Update check: when you check for updates in Settings › Updates, and at most once a day if automatic checks are on, Forkcast asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and downloads the app's installation file if you choose to install it. Nothing about you or your data is sent. GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Forkcast does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.

Backups

Backups are files you create: you choose where to save each one with Android's file picker, or pick a folder once for automatic backups. That can be on your phone, an SD card, or a cloud storage app you use; Forkcast only sees the files and the one folder you choose. Backups contain your Forkcast data and, if you choose, your meal photos, never your API keys. You can protect a backup with a password; without one, anyone with the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Health Connect

If you allow it, Forkcast reads weight, height, body fat, steps, exercise sessions and calories burned from Health Connect to keep your weight log and daily calorie budget up to date, and writes the food and water you log as nutrition and hydration records. You choose which permissions to grant and can remove them at any time in Health Connect. Health Connect data is used only for these features. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never sent anywhere except as part of the backups you make.

Android backup

Android may back up Forkcast's data to your Google account through Android's own backup service, depending on your phone's settings. API keys are excluded from it.

Exports

Spreadsheet exports (CSV) are files you create and choose where to save. They never include API keys. The separate settings export, meant for moving to a new phone, includes your API keys and is encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and nutrition labels and take meal photos. Label reading and barcode scanning happen on the device.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set and for backup problems.
  • Internet: for the services listed above.
  • Install apps: only to hand an update you downloaded from Forkcast's releases to Android's installer, which asks you to confirm.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Forkcast deletes everything it stored on the phone. Backup files you saved stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Forkcast is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
