Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026.

Summary

  • Forkcast has no servers and no accounts. The developer does not collect, receive, store, or sell any of your data.
  • Your data is stored on your phone, in backup files you create, and in Health Connect if you allow it.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs it, and it goes directly to the service involved, listed below.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What Forkcast stores on your phone

Your profile and goals (such as height, weight, age, sex used for calculations, activity level), food and water logs, meal photos, weight and activity data, settings, and the API keys you enter. API keys are encrypted on the device. You can delete everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

When data leaves your phone

  • Searching for a food or scanning a barcode sends the search text or barcode number to Open Food Facts, and to USDA FoodData Central if it is set up. Their privacy policies apply.
  • AI photo or text logging (optional, after you give permission) sends your meal photos (with location data removed), your description, and your personal-context note to the AI provider you set up, such as Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or a service you configure. That provider's privacy policy and terms apply.
  • Checking for or downloading app updates sends a standard web request to GitHub. Your IP address is visible to GitHub, like when visiting any website. GitHub's privacy statement applies.
  • Creating a backup writes your full app data to a file, wherever you choose to save it: your phone, an SD card, or a cloud folder you pick. That storage provider's policy applies, if any.
  • Android's own backup, if it is turned on in your phone's settings, copies your app data (without API keys or meal photos) to your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Turning on Health Connect writes your food and water records to Health Connect on your phone, and lets Forkcast read the records you allow, such as weight, steps, and exercise. Android Health Connect's policies apply.
  • Reporting a problem on GitHub (optional) shares only what you choose to post, such as a crash report you saved and attached. Posts on GitHub are public. GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Forkcast never sends your weight, goals, age, sex, or other health data to AI providers.

Some free AI plans let the provider use your content to improve its products, and people may review it. Read your provider's terms. Don't include faces, documents, or personal information in meal photos.

Android permissions

  • Camera: scanning barcodes and nutrition labels, and taking meal photos.
  • Internet and network status: food databases, the AI providers you set up, and update checks; waiting for a connection before sending.
  • Notifications: the reminders and alerts you turn on.
  • Running in the background and after a restart: finishing photo estimates, automatic backups, and reminders while the app is closed.
  • Biometric: unlocking saved API keys before showing or copying them.
  • Install apps (asked for when updating): installing Forkcast updates downloaded from GitHub.
  • Health Connect, only the data types you allow: reading weight, body fat, height, steps, active and total calories burned, and exercise, including older records and while the app is closed; writing nutrition and hydration.

Children

Forkcast is intended for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. API keys are encrypted with a key held by Android's secure keystore. Backups contain your data and your API keys. They are encrypted with the backup password you set, which only you have: without it, a backup can't be opened, by you or anyone else. If you choose to save a backup without encryption (Forkcast warns you first), anyone with the file can read it and use your API keys, so store it somewhere safe.

Your choices

You can view, edit, export, or delete your data in the app at any time; revoke AI or Health Connect permissions in Settings; and uninstall the app to remove all data stored on the phone. Because the developer holds no data about you, there's nothing for the developer to access, correct, or delete on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update and posted with the source code at https://github.com/djemes/forkcast. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
