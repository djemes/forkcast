Privacy policy

Forkcast for Android · Effective October 19, 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone. Forkcast has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your data. Forkcast only connects to services you choose to set up, and only to do what you asked.

What Forkcast stores on your phone

Your food log, foods and recipes, weight entries, goals, settings and meal photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. API keys you enter (for example for an AI service or the USDA food database) are encrypted on the device with the Android Keystore.

Services Forkcast connects to

Forkcast only sends data to the services below, and only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Food databases: when you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts and, if you added a key, to the USDA FoodData Central API.
  • AI photo estimates (optional): when you estimate a meal from a photo, the photo (with location and other metadata removed), your description, and any personal context you entered in Settings are sent to the AI service you configured with your own API key (for example Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or an OpenAI-compatible service). That service's own privacy policy and terms apply. Some free tiers, such as Gemini's, allow the provider to use what you send to improve its products.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): see below.
  • Health Connect (optional): see below.

Forkcast does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.

Google Drive backup

If you connect Google Drive, Forkcast asks for the drive.file permission. That lets it create, read and delete only the files it creates itself (in a "Forkcast Backups" folder). It cannot see any other files in your Drive. Forkcast uses this access only to upload your backups, list and download them when you restore, and delete old backups to keep the number of backups it stores limited. Backups contain your Forkcast data, never your API keys or meal photos, and are stored in your own Drive account. You can disconnect at any time in Forkcast (Settings › Backup & data), or remove access in your Google Account settings.

Forkcast's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Health Connect

If you allow it, Forkcast reads weight, height, body fat, steps, exercise sessions and calories burned from Health Connect to keep your weight log and daily calorie budget up to date, and writes the food and water you log as nutrition and hydration records. You choose which permissions to grant and can remove them at any time in Health Connect. Health Connect data is used only for these features. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never sent anywhere except as part of your own backups.

Android backup

Android may back up Forkcast's data to your Google account through Android's own backup service, depending on your phone's settings. API keys are excluded from it.

Exports

Exports are files you create and choose where to save. The data export never includes API keys. The separate settings export, meant for moving to a new phone, includes your API keys and is encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and nutrition labels and take meal photos. Label reading and barcode scanning happen on the device.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set and for backup problems.
  • Internet: for the services listed above.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Forkcast deletes everything it stored on the phone. Backups in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them, for example the "Forkcast Backups" folder in Drive.

Children

Forkcast is not directed at children under 13.
