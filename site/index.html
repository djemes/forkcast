Forkcast

A calorie and nutrition tracker for Android that keeps your data on your phone.

Log meals by barcode, food search, manual entry, or a photo with a short description. Forkcast tracks what you eat against a daily budget built from your goal, and shows trends for calories, weight, macros and nutrients.

Private by design

  • Everything works offline, and your log is stored on your phone.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The only services Forkcast contacts are ones you set up: food databases, the AI service you choose for photo estimates, Health Connect, and GitHub to check for updates. Backups go only where you save them.
